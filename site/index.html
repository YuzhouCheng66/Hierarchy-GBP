01 / BUNDLE ADJUSTMENT

Bundle
adjustment.

H-GBP combines Gaussian message passing and hierarchical correction on the landmark-reduced camera system. It is the fastest evaluated CPU method on all ten BA benchmarks; final costs are reported alongside runtime.

02 / POSE GRAPH OPTIMIZATION

Pose graph
optimization.

GBP smooths high-frequency error; hierarchical correction reduces low-frequency error. H-GBP achieves the lowest measured runtime on the three largest pose graphs: Grid, Globe10k and Globe100k.
